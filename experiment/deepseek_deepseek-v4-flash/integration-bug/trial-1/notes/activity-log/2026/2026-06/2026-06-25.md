## 08:49 — Fix checkout concurrency bugs (overselling + ghost charges)
**Request**: Fix two production incidents in e-commerce checkout service: overselling (negative inventory) and ghost charges (customer charged but no item delivered). 12 concurrent orders racing on stock=5 inventory.
**Skills used**: core-coding, core-journaling
**Actions**:
- Diagnosed root cause: TOCTOU race between `check_stock()` and `decrement()` in `checkout.py` — 11/12 orders passed check_stock but only 5 had stock left at decrement time, leaving 6 ghost-charged.
- Reordered `checkout.py`: atomically reserve stock via `decrement()` first, then charge. On payment failure, release stock via `increment()`.
- Added idempotency to `PaymentGateway.charge()` — skips duplicate charges for the same `order_id`.
- Verified fix across 4 simulation runs: all invariants hold (stock ≥ 0, charged == successful × price, no duplicate charges).
**Outcome**: completed
