# 2026-06-15

## Activity

- Resolved the concurrency defects in the Checkout integration service.
- Discovered that the original sequential locking across payment gateway calls was highly inefficient, and check_stock -> charge -> decrement pattern led to overselling and ghost charges.
- Implemented robust reservation-first model with atomic lock operations in `Inventory` and exception/cancellation-safe rollback using `try...finally` in `checkout`.
- Verified the solution gets `EXCELLENT` 1.0 status from the validator.
