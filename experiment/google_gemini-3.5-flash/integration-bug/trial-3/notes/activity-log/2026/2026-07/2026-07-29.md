**Request**: Fix checkout service issues with overselling (items sold when out of stock) and ghost charges (payment charged but order fails/no item delivered).
**Skills used**: core-coding, core-journaling
**Actions**:
- Modified `checkout.py` to reserve inventory (using atomic decrement) first before invoking payment charging. If payment fails or throws an exception, the stock is rolled back (incremented).
- Refactored `inventory.py` to implement absolute concurrency-safety and mutual exclusion across checks/updates by adding `asyncio.Lock()` to `Inventory` and wrapping its operations within an `async with self._lock:` block.
- Confirmed with multiple simulation runs of 12 concurrent checkouts that stock never drops below 0, charge amount matches delivered orders perfectly, and there are no duplicate/ghost charges.
