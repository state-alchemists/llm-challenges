## 18:17 — Fix checkout TOCTOU race (ghost charges + overselling)
**Request**: Fix two production bugs in e-commerce checkout — overselling (negative inventory) and ghost charges (customer charged with no item delivered).
**Skills used**: core-coding, core-journaling
**Actions**:
- Diagnosed root cause: `check_stock → charge → decrement` flow has a TOCTOU window between check and decrement, letting concurrent orders get charged then fail at decrement with no stock left.
- Reversed checkout flow to `decrement → charge → [on failure: increment]` — reserve stock first, charge second, release on payment failure.
- Verified fix across 20 stochastic simulation runs — zero violations of any invariant.
**Outcome**: completed
