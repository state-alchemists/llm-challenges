# 2026-07-29

## Fixed: E-Commerce Checkout Concurrency Bugs

**Problem**: Two production bugs in concurrent checkout flow — overselling (inventory going negative) and ghost charges (customer charged but no item delivered).

**Root cause**: TOCTOU races between stock check, payment, and stock decrement; unprotected shared state in `decrement()` and `PaymentGateway.charge()`.

**Fix**: Atomic reserve-before-charge pattern with `asyncio.Lock`:

- `Inventory.reserve(quantity)` — atomic check-and-decrement (single point of truth for availability)
- `Inventory.release(quantity)` — rollback reserved stock on payment failure
- Locked critical sections in `Inventory.decrement()`, `Inventory.increment()`, and `PaymentGateway.charge()`

**Files**: `checkout.py`, `inventory.py`, `payments.py`

**Verification**: 10/10 runs clean with 12 concurrent orders, stock=5, 25% payment failure rate — no negative stock, no charge/success mismatch, no duplicate charges.
