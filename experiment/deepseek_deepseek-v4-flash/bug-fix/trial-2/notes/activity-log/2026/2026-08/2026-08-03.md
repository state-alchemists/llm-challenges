- 11:15 — Fixed two bugs in the job queue (bug-fix challenge). (1) `job_queue.py:dequeue` had `await asyncio.sleep(0.01)` between the `pending` check and the `processing` claim — a check-then-act race letting every worker claim the same job. (2) `worker.py` caught exceptions but never called `queue.fail()`, so crashed jobs stayed `processing` forever (vanishing failures). Added `queue.fail(job["id"], str(e))`.
