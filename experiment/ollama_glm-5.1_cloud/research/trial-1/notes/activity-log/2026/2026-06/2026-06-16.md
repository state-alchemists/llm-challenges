## ADR-001: Notification Subsystem Architecture

**What**: Wrote Architecture Decision Record comparing Redis Streams vs Apache Kafka for the notification subsystem of a SaaS project management platform.

**Outcome**: Recommended Redis Streams. Key reasons:
- Redis already in production — meets 2-week setup constraint
- 6-person team with no Kafka experience — operational burden of Kafka is too high
- Throughput sufficient for current (500 req/s) and 10x growth target
- Exactly-once for billing achieved via PostgreSQL outbox/dedup pattern
