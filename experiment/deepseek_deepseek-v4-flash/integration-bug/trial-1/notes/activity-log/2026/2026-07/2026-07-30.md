# 2026-07-30

## 07:42 — Fix checkout concurrency bugs (ghost charges + overselling race)
**Request**: Fix two production bugs in e-commerce checkout — overselling (TOCTOU race on inventory) and ghost charges (payment succeeds but no item delivered).

**Skills used**: core-coding, core-journaling

**Actions**:
- Diagnosed root cause in `checkout.py`: `check_stock()` and `decrement()` were separate coroutines with an `await` gap, allowing concurrent orders to all pass the stock check before any could decrement; plus the charge-before-decrement ordering meant successful charges with no delivered item.
- Added `asyncio.Lock` and `async def reserve(self, quantity)` to `Inventory` — atomically checks stock and decrements under a single lock, eliminating the TOCTOU race.
- Refactored `checkout.py` to reserve-first → charge-second: stock is decremented (reserved) before payment. On payment failure, the reservation is released via `inventory.increment()`.
- Added `refund()` method to `PaymentGateway` for defensive completeness.
- Verified: 10/10 simulation runs, no ghost charges (`$500` charged always = `5 × $100`), no overselling (stock flat at 0), no duplicate charges.

**Outcome**: completed
