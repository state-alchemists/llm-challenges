## 02:50 — Fix checkout TOCTOU race + ghost charge bug
**Request**: Fix two production bugs in e-commerce checkout — overselling (inventory goes negative) and ghost charges (customers charged but no item delivered).
**Skills used**: core-coding, core-journaling
**Actions**:
- Diagnosed root cause: `checkout()` checked stock (check_stock), then charged (charge), then decremented (decrement). Race window between check_stock and decrement allowed over-selling. Charging before decrementing meant failed decrements still recorded a charge — ghost charges.
- Rewrote `checkout()` in `checkout.py`: decrement inventory FIRST (atomic check+subtract, no race), charge only after reservation secured, increment stock back if charge fails.
- Removed the now-redundant `check_stock()` call from the checkout flow (the decrement guard is the single authoritative stock check).
- Validated: 30/30 concurrent simulations pass with zero errors (no negative stock, no charge mismatches, no duplicate charges).
