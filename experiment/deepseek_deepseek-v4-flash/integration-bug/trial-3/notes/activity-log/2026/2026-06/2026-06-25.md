# 2026-06-25

## Fix: Concurrent overselling and ghost charges in e-commerce checkout

**Context:** Integration bug trial-3 — e-commerce checkout service with concurrent orders. 12 concurrent orders competing for 5 items of stock, with 25% simulated payment failure rate.

**Root cause:** Checkout sequence was check-stock → charge → decrement. Between check-stock and decrement, concurrent orders could all see sufficient stock (TOCTOU race). When decrement failed after charge, the customer was charged but never delivered — ghost charge. Concurrent decrements could also both pass the `_stock >= quantity` check before either wrote, causing overselling.

**Fix:**
- Added `asyncio.Lock` to `Inventory` in `inventory.py`
- Added `try_reserve(quantity)` — atomic check+decrement under lock
- Added `release_reservation(quantity)` — returns reserved stock to pool (compensation for failed payment)
- Restructured `checkout()` in `checkout.py` to: reserve → charge → release-on-failure

**Verification:** 10 simulation runs, each with 12 orders × 5 stock:
- No negative stock
- No ghost charges (total charged always equals successful × price)
- No duplicate charges
- Items sold never exceeds initial stock (5)

**Files changed:**
- `/workdir/inventory.py` — added lock + `try_reserve` + `release_reservation`
- `/workdir/checkout.py` — restructured to reserve-first workflow
