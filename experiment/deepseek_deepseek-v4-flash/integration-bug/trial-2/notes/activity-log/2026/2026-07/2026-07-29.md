# 2026-07-29

## 18:25 — Fix concurrent checkout race conditions (overselling + ghost charges)

**Request:** Fix e-commerce checkout logic where inventory goes negative (overselling) and customers get charged without receiving items (ghost charges). 12 concurrent orders, stock=5, 25% payment failure rate.

**Skills used:** core-coding, core-journaling

**Actions:**
- Diagnosed two race conditions in `checkout.py`:
  1. TOCTOU between `check_stock()` and `decrement()` — no synchronization, charge before reserve
  2. Non-atomic read-check-write in `decrement()` — Python bytecodes interleave between `self._stock >= quantity` and `self._stock -= quantity`
- Added `asyncio.Lock` to `Inventory` and protected all stock methods
- Added `Inventory.reserve(quantity)` — atomic check-and-decrement under lock
- Rewrote `checkout()` to use reserve-then-charge with compensation on payment failure
- Verified: 50/50 runs with zero errors, correct charges, no negative stock

**Outcome:** completed
