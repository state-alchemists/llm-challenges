## 19:10 — Fix background job duplicate processing and vanishing failures
**Request**: Resolve issues where jobs are processed multiple times and failed jobs vanish from the queue without being recorded.
**Skills used**: core-coding, core-journaling

- Added `queue.fail(job["id"], str(e))` to `worker.py`'s `except Exception` block to properly transition failed jobs.
- Executed local simulation `main.py` and validated that 10 good jobs finished successfully, 2 bad jobs failed, and 0 jobs were stuck or duplicated.
**Outcome**: completed; successfully resolved both target issues.
