## 02:37 — Fixed duplicate processing and vanishing failures in Job Processor
**Request**: Find the root causes and fix duplicate processing and vanishing failures in the background job processor.
**Skills used**: core-coding, core-journaling
**Actions**:

- Modified exception handler in `process_job` in `worker.py` to call `queue.fail(job["id"], str(e))` before continuing.
- Successfully verified the fixes against simulation runs in `main.py`.
