## 18:18 — Fixed Concurrency Defects in Checkout Flow
**Request**: Fix checkout flow to prevent overselling and ghost charges under concurrent load.
**Skills used**: core-coding, core-journaling
**Actions**:
- Discovered race condition in `checkout.py` where checkouts checked stock before decrementing, leading to overselling and ghost charges when payments failed.
- Redesigned checkout to use a reserve-then-commit/rollback pattern.
- Modified `checkout.py` to decrement stock first to reserve the items.
- If payment fails, return the items to stock using `inventory.increment(quantity)`.
**Outcome**: completed; simulation runs concurrently and cleanly, with zero negative stock or mismatched charges.
