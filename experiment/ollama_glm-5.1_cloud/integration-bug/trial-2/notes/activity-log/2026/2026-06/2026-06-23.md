# 2026-06-23

## integration-bug: Fix TOCTOU race in checkout

- **Task**: Fix two production incidents in e-commerce checkout service — overselling (inventory goes negative) and ghost charges (customer charged but no item delivered).
- **Root cause**: TOCTOU race between `check_stock()` and `decrement()` in `checkout.py`. The `check_stock → charge → decrement` flow allowed concurrent coroutines to all pass the stock check, get charged, and then some fail at decrement.
- **Fix applied**:
  - `inventory.py`: Added `asyncio.Lock` to `__init__`; wrapped `decrement()` and `increment()` with `async with self._lock`.
  - `checkout.py`: Changed flow from check→charge→decrement to **reserve (decrement) → charge → rollback (increment) on failure**. Removed the separate `check_stock()` call.
- **Verification**: Ran simulation 10× with default params (5 stock, 12 orders) — zero errors. Stress-tested 20× with 10 stock, 50 orders, 30% failure rate — all passed (no overselling, no ghost charges, no duplicates).
- **Public interfaces preserved**: `Inventory.check_stock`, `Inventory.decrement`, `Inventory.increment`, `Inventory.stock`, `PaymentGateway.charge` — all unchanged in signature.
- **See also**: [asyncio TOCTOU checkout note](../../../technical/asyncio-toctou-checkout.md)
